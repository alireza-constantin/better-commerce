# 14 — Campaign drafting and review

**What to build:** Staff can draft a Campaign, attach a Promotion version, select Customers or a saved segment, preview SMS encoding/units/cost, and send a restricted test.

**Blocked by:** 11 — Versioned transactional templates; 13 — Saved Customer segments.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Drafts support individual, all messageable, and saved-segment audiences.
- [ ] Preview shows encoding, four-segment limit, recipient count, exclusions, and estimated units.
- [ ] Promotion facts are versioned at review time.
- [ ] Draft content remains editable until final confirmation.
