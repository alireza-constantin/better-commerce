# 08 — Direct Customer messaging

**What to build:** Authorized support staff can compose, preview, estimate, confirm, and track one Direct message from a Customer workspace.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Purpose-based provider routing; 07 — Customer directory and detail workspace.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Direct messages require the exact send permission and a human-readable purpose.
- [ ] Test delivery is restricted to the staff member's verified mobile or configured test number.
- [ ] The UI shows queued/accepted/failure outcomes without raw provider internals.
- [ ] Customer and staff message history is auditable and idempotent.
