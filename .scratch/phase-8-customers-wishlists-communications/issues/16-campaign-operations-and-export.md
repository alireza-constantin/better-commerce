# 16 — Campaign cancellation, reconciliation, and export

**What to build:** Staff can cancel remaining Campaign work, inspect recipient-level outcomes, review unknown deliveries, duplicate historical Campaigns, and export an audited CSV.

**Blocked by:** 15 — Campaign scheduling and delivery.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Cancellation stops unclaimed work without pretending accepted messages were recalled.
- [ ] Unknown outcomes remain visible and are never automatically resent.
- [ ] Historical Campaigns are immutable and can only be duplicated into drafts.
- [ ] CSV export enforces permissions, masking, and audit facts.
